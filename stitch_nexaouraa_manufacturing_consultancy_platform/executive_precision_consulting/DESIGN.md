## Brand & Style

The design system projects unshakeable authority, engineering rigor, and executive prestige for Tier-1 automotive and discrete manufacturing consulting. It directly reflects the core competencies of global manufacturing frameworks—specifically IATF 16949, Total Quality Management (QMS/IMS), APQP, PPAP, and Core Quality Tools (FMEA, SPC, MSA).

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Executive Stature & Trust:** Grounded in an authoritative deep executive navy paired with metallic industrial gold accents, signaling boardroom-level advisory and enterprise-grade reliability.
- **Process Accuracy & Mastery:** Precision emerald green communicates compliance, zero-defect philosophies, and continuous operational improvement (Kaizen, 6-Sigma).
- **Target Audience:** Plant Heads, Managing Directors, Quality VPs, Operations Directors, and Tier-1/Tier-2 automotive supplier leadership seeking tangible audits, certifications, and measurable yield improvements.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Precision with Industrial Tactility
The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate** structure with **Precision Industrial Accents**:
- Geometric architectural layout systems mirroring rigorous technical drawings and shop floor audit scorecards.
- Crisp, hairline metallic borders and subtle linear grids reminiscent of precision micrometer scales and engineering schematics.
- High-contrast card surfaces, clear data visualization hierarchies for compliance metrics, and prominent conversion touchpoints designed for direct executive engagement via scheduled advisory and direct WhatsApp consultation.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the official emblem, balancing deep institutional authority with operational vitality and metallic distinction.

### Primary Palette: Deep Executive Navy (`#0F2537`)
- **Usage:** Anchor elements, primary navigation bars, executive headers, primary button backgrounds, and strong typographic display titles.
- **Tonal Variants:**
  - `navy-dark` (`#0A192F`): Deep grounding canvas for dark-mode components and high-impact hero banners.
  - `navy-light` (`#1B3B54`): Interactive states, active row states in audit tables, and subtle container fills.

### Secondary Accent: Precision Emerald Green (`#0E6A38`)
- **Usage:** Quality verification tags, audit pass indicators, continuous improvement KPI spikes, and direct WhatsApp executive integration.
- **Tonal Variants:**
  - `emerald-vibrant` (`#15803D`): Action badges, success notifications, and verified badge indicators.
  - `emerald-surface` (`#ECFDF5`): Soft pill backgrounds for QMS status and audit checkpoints.

### Highlight & Prestige: Industrial Gold (`#C59A27`)
- **Usage:** Executive award callouts, star badges, accent dividers, key consultation CTA borders, and high-priority KPI value markers.
- **Tonal Variants:**
  - `gold-bright` (`#E5B23A`): Hover states and micro-highlights on dark navy backgrounds.
  - `gold-warm` (`#D4A017`): Key metric callouts and quotation border accents.
  - `gold-subtle` (`#FEF9C3`): Alert surfaces and core audit focus tags.

### Neutrals & Structural Grays
- **Background Light:** `#F8FAFC` (Canvas foundation) and `#FFFFFF` (Elevated card containers).
- **Surface Accent Gray:** `#F1F5F9` for secondary cards, table header backgrounds, and divider hairlines.
- **Text Primary:** `#0F2537` (Extreme legibility on light mode; avoids muddy pure black).
- **Text Secondary:** `#475569` (Subtitles, audit descriptors, parameter metadata).
- **Borders & Rules:** `#E2E8F0` with subtle highlight overlays of `#CBD5E1`.

## Typography

The typographical architecture unites **Montserrat** for architectural, resolute structural headings with **Inter** for technical precision and extreme data legibility.

### Display & Section Heads (Montserrat)
Headlines project institutional stature. All section badges, core domain titles (e.g., *IATF 16949 IMPLEMENTATION*, *APQP & PPAP SUPPORT*), and hero banners use Montserrat in bold/extra-bold weights with tight negative letter tracking to reinforce stability and solid engineering.

### Body & Data Records (Inter)
Body copy, analytical findings, metric explanations, and audit reports require neutral objectivity. Inter provides uniform optical proportions, robust numerals for audit statistics, and crystal-clear clarity across high-density enterprise layouts.

### Special Typography Roles
- **Technical Badges (`label-technical`):** Rendered in uppercase with `0.08em` letter spacing for manufacturing credentials (e.g., `ISO 9001:2015`, `VDA 6.3`, `SIX SIGMA DMAIC`).
- **Audit Metric Values:** Large data readouts (e.g., `99.8% FTT`, `0 PPM`) utilize Montserrat ExtraBold with tabular numbers enabled to maintain vertical alignment in scorecard grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a disciplined 12-column grid system reflecting industrial modularity and operational cleanliness.

### Grid Rhythm & Columnar Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns with a maximum canvas width of `1280px`, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) safe outer margins. Standard consulting modules span 4 columns (3-card service row) or 6 columns (2-column audit comparison).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. 3-card service structures reflow to a 2-column stacked configuration.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Cards collapse into unified linear vertical stacks with tactile thumb targets.

### Spacing Density Principles
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Internal pill padding, icon-to-label inline offsets, and table cell vertical gaps.
- **Component Padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Internal card padding and form inputs to ensure breathing room without bloating technical density.
- **Structural Separation (`space-xl` and multipliers):** Generous 4rem to 6rem vertical separation between major consulting tracks to maintain an executive, deliberate pace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes architectural depth, relying on crisp surface layering, razor-thin borders, and selective directional shadowing rather than heavy ambient blurs.

### Surface Tiers & Layering
1. **Base Layer (Ground):** `#F8FAFC` creates a cool, pristine workshop/executive campus backdrop.
2. **Structural Container (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` cards bordered with `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Depth is articulated through a clean perimeter line rather than dark shadows.
3. **Elevated Card (Level 2):** Subtle resting elevation using a sharp, tinted industrial shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.04)`.
4. **Interactive Focus / Active Hover (Level 3):**
   `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.10), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(197, 154, 39, 0.08); transform: translateY(-2px);`
   The faint gold-tinted glow on hover signifies premier status.

### Precision Accents
- **Metallic Edge Rule:** Cards representing flagship services (e.g., *IATF 16949 Turnkey Advisory*) incorporate a top accent border: `3px solid #C59A27` or a left accent bar: `4px solid #0E6A38`.
- **Dividers:** Fine continuous hairpins rendered in `#E2E8F0` with occasional center diamond or star ornaments referencing the logo mark.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft (`1`)** shape language, communicating engineering precision, structural stability, and rigorous industrial standards.

### Geometric Rules
- **Structural Cards & Panels:** Bound by strict `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) radii. This eliminates playful softness in favor of machined, calibrated edges.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Standardized at `0.25rem` (4px) corner rounding to maintain alignment with technical input panels.
- **Badges & Status Chips:** `0.25rem` (4px) for technical codes (e.g., `APQP`, `FMEA`), preserving the look of stamped steel asset tags or quality control decals.
- **Conversion Touchpoints:** WhatsApp executive floating CTAs and core round emblem badges utilize circular geometry (`50%`) to create contrast against the angular grid.
